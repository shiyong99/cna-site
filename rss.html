How to access RSS

1. Install a news reader

Download a news reader that can display RSS content — many are free, as apps or in-browser.

2. Select the content you want

Copy the feed URL for the category you want and paste it into your reader.

3. Subscribe

Updates will be delivered to your reader whenever there's new content.
